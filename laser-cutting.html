Laser Cutting

Laser cutting is a precise manufacturing process that uses a laser to cut and engrave materials with high accuracy. It is widely used in various industries for its precision and versatility in handling different materials.

Glowforge Laser Cutter: The Glowforge is a popular desktop laser cutting and engraving machine known for its ease of use and high-quality results. It features a built-in camera system for precise material alignment and real-time visualization of the cutting path.

The Glowforge can handle materials such as wood, acrylic, leather, and more. It is ideal for creating intricate designs, personalized items, and functional prototypes.

Laser cutting with the Glowforge provides an efficient and versatile solution for a wide range of creative and industrial applications.
