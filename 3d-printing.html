3D Printing

3D printing, or additive manufacturing, is a transformative technology that creates three-dimensional objects by building up layers of material based on a digital design. This process has revolutionized industries from prototyping and manufacturing to healthcare and creative arts.

FDM (Fused Deposition Modeling): FDM is one of the most widely used 3D printing methods, known for its accessibility and versatility. It works by extruding melted thermoplastic filament through a heated nozzle, layer by layer, to build up the object.

DLP (Digital Light Processing): DLP is a high-resolution 3D printing technology that utilizes a digital light projector to cure liquid resin layer by layer. This method provides finer detail and smoother surface finishes compared to FDM.

Both FDM and DLP technologies offer unique advantages tailored to different needs. FDM is ideal for larger, functional prototypes and low-cost production, while DLP is preferred for high-detail, small-scale applications.
