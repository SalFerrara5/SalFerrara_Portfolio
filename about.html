About Me

Hi, I’m Sal Ferrara, an Electrical Engineering student at Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute with a passion for building, designing, and problem-solving.

I enjoy creating projects that combine electronics, embedded systems, CAD, and hands-on fabrication into practical real-world solutions.

Engineering & Projects

My main interests lie in embedded systems, microcontrollers, and hands-on hardware design. I enjoy working at the intersection of software and electronics, where theoretical concepts become real systems that can be tested, improved, and refined.

Many of my projects focus on reliability, practical functionality, and thoughtful design rather than unnecessary complexity.

Community & Brotherhood

I’m a proud member of the Theta Chapter of Chi Phi Fraternity, where leadership, brotherhood, and service have played a major role in shaping my college experience.

Through philanthropy events, chapter leadership, and teamwork, I’ve developed stronger communication, accountability, and organizational skills.

Outdoors & Personal Interests

Outside of engineering, I enjoy fishing, working on DIY projects, and spending time outdoors. These activities give me a balance away from technical work while still keeping me hands-on and problem-solving oriented.

Looking Forward

I’m always looking to expand my skills, take on new challenges, and continue learning through projects, collaboration, and real-world engineering experience.

This portfolio serves as a collection of my work, interests, and growth as both an engineer and builder.
