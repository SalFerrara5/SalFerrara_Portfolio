About Me

Hello! I’m Sal Ferrara, a passionate student and creator with a deep interest in engineering and design. Currently, I am studying Electrical Engineering at Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute, where I focus on projects that blend technology and creativity. Outside of academics, I enjoy working on personal projects involving 3D printing, electronics, and custom designs. In my free time, you might find me fishing or working on various DIY projects. I'm also a proud member of the Theta Chapter of the Chi Phi Fraternity; the oldest social fraternity in the USA; where we take pride in doing community service and philanthropy work for charities like Saint Baldrick's childhood cancer research and RAINN. This website is a showcase of my work, interests, and the journey I’m on.

Engineering Interests

I am particularly interested in embedded systems, microcontrollers, and hands on hardware design. I enjoy working at the intersection of software and electronics, where theoretical concepts are translated into real world systems. Many of my projects focus on practical applications, reliability, and thoughtful design rather than unnecessary complexity.

Projects & Building

A large portion of my time is spent designing, building, and refining personal projects. This includes 3D printing, electronics prototyping, and creating custom enclosures and assemblies. I enjoy the full process from initial concept and CAD design through testing and iteration, and I take pride in building things that are both functional and well executed.

Fraternity & Brotherhood

I am a proud Brother of the Theta Chapter of the Chi Phi Fraternity, the oldest social fraternity in the United States. Being part of Chi Phi has played a significant role in my college experience, emphasizing brotherhood, leadership, and service. Our chapter actively participates in philanthropy and community service, supporting organizations such as Saint Baldrick’s childhood cancer research and RAINN. The fraternity has helped reinforce accountability, leadership skills, and lifelong friendships.

Outdoors, Hunting, & Fishing

I spend much of my free time outdoors, particularly hunting and fishing. These activities have helped develop patience, discipline, and self-reliance, while also fostering a strong respect for nature and conservation. Time spent outdoors provides balance to my academic and technical pursuits and reinforces skills that carry over into everyday life.

Values & Patriotism

I hold strong values centered around personal responsibility, hard work, and patriotism. I have a deep respect and appreciation for the men and women of the United States military and first responders who dedicate themselves to serving and protecting others. These principles influence how I approach my education, my projects, and how I conduct myself both personally and professionally.

Looking Forward

I am always looking to expand my skills, take on new challenges, and continue learning both inside and outside the classroom. This website serves as a living portfolio that documents my work, interests, and growth as an engineer, builder, and individual.
