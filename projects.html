FerriteEvo:

FerriteEvo is one of my first major projects. It is a custom designed and built 3D printer. I designed it to be a large scale smart printer that I can control from anywhere using my phone or laptop with an internet connection. It is a crossXY cartesian style FDM printer, with a 500x500x500mm print area. It is able to auto-level and scan the print bed, creating a mesh of the bed used to adjut the z height throughout the print for optimal bed adhesion. It also has an onboard webcam to monitor prints remotely and record timelapses.
