Fishing Gyzmo

Fishing Gyzmo – A portable fishing companion that tracks your catches and displays them on a mini-map. Built on an ESP32 with GPS, it logs location, time, and catch details to an SD card. Features a simple touchscreen interface using LVGL for easy navigation and real-time position tracking. Designed for anglers who want a tech edge without bulky gear.

Ferrite Evo

Ferrite Evo – A large-format 3D printer of my own design with a 500 mm × 500 mm × 500 mm build volume. The system runs on Klipper with Mainsail for remote monitoring and control. Planned features include automatic bed leveling with eddy current scanning, an onboard webcam for timelapses and failure detection, and full remote access over the internet. This is one of my most ambitious builds and is currently still under development.
